## Job 1 — Protect the app · 2 minutes · do this first

1. Open your **Projects** folder and double-click **`skydog-backup-keys.command`**
2. It asks you to make up a password. Type one (12+ characters).
3. **Write that password on a piece of paper.** Not on the computer.
4. It puts a file on your Desktop. Copy that file onto a **USB stick**.
5. Delete it from the Desktop. Keep the stick somewhere other than your laptop bag.

**Why:** There is one file on your Mac that proves this app is yours. Google uses
it to recognize every update you'll ever publish. If your Mac dies and that file
is gone, SkyDog GPS can never be updated again — not by anyone, ever. This makes
a spare copy that's useless to a thief.
