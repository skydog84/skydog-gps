Common questions

What does SkyDog All Access include?
Every feature pack for one auto-renewing subscription — $4.99 per month, or $44.99 per year: Fishing (real DNR lake depth contours, NOAA charts, depth-at-tap, launch + parking intel, local rules & records), Drone Mode, and ORV Trails — plus every pack we add to All Access later. Ads pay for the free app; an All Access subscription removes them and unlocks every pack. Party Mode and push-to-talk are free for your whole crew — no subscription needed.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SkyDog All Access → Cancel. You keep the packs until the end of the paid period. Web purchases: use the link on your Stripe receipt or email us and we’ll cancel it for you.

I subscribed (or bought the original Fishing Pack) on another device / reinstalled — how do I get it back?
It's tied to your Apple ID. Reinstall the app and it restores automatically, or tap "Restore Purchases" in the 🎒 store sheet — or email us and we'll help. Early buyers of the original one-time Fishing Pack keep those features forever.

Where are my saved trips?
Right on your device — no account needed. Use Share or GPX export to get a trip out into Google Earth, OnX, Gaia, or AllTrails.

Why does the app ask for my location?
Only to show you on the map and track your adventures while you're using it. Your location stays on your phone — with one exception: if you start or join a Buddy Trip, your live position is shared with that trip's members until you end it. See our privacy policy and terms of use.
