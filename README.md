## What it does — free, forever

- 🆘 **Safety & Night Ops** — emergency screen with MGRS/USNG grid coordinates for 911 and a rescuer link, overdue timer with auto-email, 🌙 red-light night mode, darkness timeline, aurora forecast, 🐟 solunar activity meter
- 🌎 **World Data** — live weather radar, NWS alerts (including marine), USGS river gauges, active wildfires
- 🎉 **Party Mode** — live crew map + push-to-talk voice clips + Footprints/Solitude. Free for the whole crew.
- 🌄 **3D Terrain** — full 3D flyover with a real horizon and aerial perspective
- 📓 **Trip Log + Replay** — every adventure saved on-phone and replayed on the map
- 🏃 **Activity Engine** — 19 activities, knots on the water, auto-loaded map layers
- 📍 **My Spots** — private pins, phone-local · 👥 **Buddy Trips** — room-code live location sharing, consent-first
- 🗺️ **The classics** — three map styles (Streets, Topo, Satellite), one-tap discovery of trails, beaches & launches, GPX share (opens in Google Earth, OnX, Gaia, AllTrails), Map Key, "What's this?"
- 📴 **No accounts, no sign-up.** Your GPS tracks stay on your phone (Buddy Trips relay live location only while you choose to share).

## All Access — $4.99/mo · $44.99/yr

- 🦌 **Hunt Intelligence** — wind & thermals per stand, Recovery Mode
- 📷 **Trail Cam Hub** — private email ingest, photo timeline, triage tags, conditions cross-ref
- 🚁 **Drone** — FAA airspace grid, wind/temp HUD with a go/no-go light
- 🛞 **ORV Trails** — Michigan DNR snowmobile/ORV/motorcycle network + closures
- 🎣 **Fishing** — DNR lake depth contours, NOAA charts, depth-at-tap, launch & parking intel

Party Mode and push-to-talk stay free for everyone — no subscription needed.

## Put it on your phone

1. Open **https://skydoggps.com** in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**
3. It installs with the SkyDog AI logo as its icon and runs full-screen like a real app

Coming to the App Store and Google Play — [testers get it first](https://skydoggps.com/testers.html).

## Tech

Single-file HTML/JS with a custom-built zero-dependency map engine (canvas tile renderer,
pinch-zoom, custom markers & popups, WebGL 3D terrain). Map data © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
contributors; topo tiles © OpenTopoMap; satellite imagery © Esri. Verified by a **523-test**
automated browser suite (simulated GPS, tracking, GPX export, offline resilience, 3D horizon
invariants).
