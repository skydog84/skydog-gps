FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your adventure tracker for trails, beaches, waves & wheels — powered by SkyDog AI.

SkyDog GPS puts you on the map and keeps you moving. Pick your map, find your
spot, hit Start, and go. No account, no sign-up, no nonsense.

THREE MAPS, ONE TAP
• Streets — clean and fast for getting around
• Topo — contour lines for hunting land, hills and trail country
• Satellite — see the actual terrain, shorelines and structure

FIND SOMETHING TO DO
One-tap discovery drops pins on Trails, Beaches, Boat Launches, Waterfalls,
Overlooks, Camping, Picnic spots, Parking, Fishing spots and more — all near
you, anywhere in the world. Tap a pin and Drive There hands off to navigation.

TRACK YOUR ADVENTURE
Hike, Boat, Kayak, Bike, Wave Runner, 4-Wheeler, Snowmobile and more — with
live time, miles, current speed and max speed on a clean HUD. Save the trip,
then share a summary or export a GPX file that opens in Google Earth, OnX,
Gaia and AllTrails.

BUDDY TRIPS
Share a live trip with the people you are out with, so everyone can see where
everyone is.

WHAT'S THIS?
Tap anything on the map — SkyDog identifies it. A Map Key explains every color
and symbol on whichever map style you are on.

🎒 SKYDOG ALL ACCESS (optional subscription)
• Fishing Pack — real DNR lake depth contours, NOAA charts on the Great Lakes,
  depth-at-tap, launch intel, local rules, records and stocking info
• Drone Pack — FAA airspace ceilings, recreational flying fields, wind and a
  go / no-go flight check
• ORV Trails — Michigan snowmobile and ORV network with open/closed status,
  pit stops and staging
One low subscription unlocks every pack — and the map stays free.

🏡 PROPERTY LINES (early preview)
Flip on the Property Lines layer and tap a parcel to see boundaries, owner and
acreage. Live in our first preview areas now — wider coverage is on the way.

FREE FOREVER
GPS tracking, all three maps, discovery, saving and GPX export stay 100% free.
SkyDog GPS is ad-supported and ads are shown to everyone.

A SkyDog AI app. Made with love. Find your spot. 🐾
