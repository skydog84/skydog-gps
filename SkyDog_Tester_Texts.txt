TIPS

• Recruit 15, not 12. Two or three people will flake, and if you drop
  below 12 at any point the 14-day clock restarts from zero.

• Text people one at a time. A personal text gets a yes; a mass
  group blast gets ignored.

• Ask in person when you can. Nearly impossible to say no to.

• Best people to ask first: anyone who's ever asked you about the app,
  anyone who hunts / rides / fishes, and family.

• Write down the date the 12th person opts in. That is the day your
  clock actually starts, and you'll want to know it exactly.
