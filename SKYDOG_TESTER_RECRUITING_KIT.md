## TIER 3 — tester-swap communities (filler, only if still short of 15)

These are devs and app-testing hobbyists who sign up for each other's closed tests.
They count toward Google's number, but they're strangers — expect zero real feedback,
and a few may uninstall early, so overshoot by 3–4 if you lean on this tier.

- **r/AndroidClosedTesting** (Reddit) — post flair/format rules are in the sidebar. Title like:
  `[Seeking testers] SkyDog GPS — outdoor/hunting maps app (US) — will test yours back`
  Body: two sentences about the app + the skydoggps.com/testers link + offer to
  reciprocal-test their app (that's the currency there).
- **testerscommunity.com** — organized tester exchange for exactly this Google
  requirement. Read their current terms before leaning on it (some tiers are paid).

*(FYI: Google's official minimum dropped from 20 to ~12 in mid-2025 per dev-community
reports, but your Play Console will state the exact number for your account when the
app is created — 15 stays our safe target.)*
