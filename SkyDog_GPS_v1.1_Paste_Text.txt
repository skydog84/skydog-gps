4) REVIEW NOTES for the v1.1 submission (App Review notes box)
--------------------------------------------------------------
SkyDog GPS is a fully client-side maps/tracking app. No account or login is
required anywhere in the app. Location is used only on-device (when-in-use)
to show the user on the map and record their trip; it is never uploaded.
Purchases: the SkyDog All Access subscription
(com.skydog.skygps.allaccess.monthly) unlocks the Fishing, Drone and ORV
feature packs. The legacy non-consumable com.skydog.skygps.fishingpack is no
longer offered for sale but is still honored via Restore Purchases. Ads are
shown to all users; no purchase removes ads. Restore Purchases is available
on the purchase sheet. The Property Lines layer is an early preview with
limited coverage areas, and the app says so in-product.
