Put it on your phone

SkyDog GPS installs straight from the web. No store required.

  1. Open skydoggps.com in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. It installs with the SkyDog logo and runs full-screen like a real app. Your GPS tracks stay on the phone. No account, no sign-up.
