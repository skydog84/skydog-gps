📞 CALL 911 📱 TEXT SOS — message is pre-filled
SkyDog helps you share your location in an emergency. It is not a rescue service and it is no substitute for calling 911, a personal locator beacon, or your phone's satellite SOS. The rescuer link needs cell signal to update; your last position stays visible for 24 hours, then it's gone. Texting 911 works in many US counties — call if you can, text if you can't.
