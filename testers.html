How it works, start to finish

  1. Send us your Gmail. Hit the button up top — it writes the email for you.
  2. Wait for the link. We add you to the tester list and send it over. This may take a few days if the test hasn't opened yet.
  3. Open the link on your Android phone and tap "Become a tester."
  4. Install SkyDog GPS from the Google Play link on that same page.
  5. Leave it installed for 14 days. We'll message you the day you're free and clear.
The one thing that actually matters: please don't uninstall it early. Google counts 14 unbroken days — if one person drops out on day 12, the clock resets to zero for everybody. One text from us on day 7, one on day 14, and you're done.

So what is SkyDog GPS?

A GPS and mapping app for people who spend time outside — trails, beaches, hunting land, boats, sleds and four-wheelers. Three map styles including topo and satellite, one-tap discovery for trails and boat launches and waterfalls near you, live trip tracking with speed and distance, and GPX export that opens in Google Earth, OnX, Gaia or AllTrails.

It's free, there's no account and no sign-up, and it's already live on the iPhone App Store. Google Play is the last step.

Fair warning about location

It's a GPS app, so it asks for your location — that's how it puts you on the map. Your location stays on your phone. The one exception is Buddy Trips: if you start or join one, your live position is shared with the people on that trip until you end it. Nothing is shared otherwise, and there's no account tied to any of it. The full privacy policy is right here.
