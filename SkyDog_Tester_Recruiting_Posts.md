## RULES THAT KEEP YOU OUT OF TROUBLE

- **Ask 15, not 12.** People flake. Dropping below 12 restarts the 14-day clock
  from zero for everybody.
- **Log the date the 12th person opts in.** That is day 1. Not the day you
  created the test.
- Never say "leave a 5-star review" — asking testers for reviews violates
  Play policy.
- Never offer to pay individuals for reviews or installs.
- Testing back is normal and fine. Fake accounts are not — don't stuff the list
  with your own Gmails.
